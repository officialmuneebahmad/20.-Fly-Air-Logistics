International Commercial Terms (Incoterms), established by the International Chamber of Commerce (ICC), govern cross-border commerce by defining who pays for shipping, who bears insurance responsibility, and precisely where risk transfers from seller to buyer.

Misunderstanding Incoterms is among the leading causes of financial disputes, unforeseen demurrage charges, and insurance claim denials for Pakistani businesses.

## 1. Top 4 Incoterms Used in Pakistan International Trade

### 1. EXW (Ex Works) - Buyer Takes Maximum Control
- **The Setup:** The seller makes the goods available at their warehouse, mill, or factory (e.g., in Faisalabad or Gujranwala).
- **Responsibility:** The buyer arranges local transport, Pakistani export customs clearance, ocean/air carriage, and destination import clearance.
- **When to Use:** Suitable when international buyers have established forwarding networks in Pakistan and want total visibility over shipping costs.

### 2. FOB (Free on Board) - The Exporter's Standard
- **The Setup:** The Pakistani seller delivers the goods past the ship's rail at Karachi Port Trust (KPT) or Port Qasim and completes all Pakistan export customs formalities.
- **Responsibility:** The overseas buyer pays the ocean freight, marine cargo insurance, and handles import clearance at the destination port (e.g., Felixstowe or Los Angeles).
- **Why it is Popular:** The exporter avoids liability for unpredictable ocean freight rate fluctuations while maintaining control over local production and port handling.

### 3. CIF (Cost, Insurance and Freight) - Enhanced Seller Service
- **The Setup:** The Pakistani exporter pays for transportation up to the foreign destination port and procures marine cargo insurance (minimum Institute Cargo Clauses C or agreed A coverage).
- **Risk Transfer:** Critical distinction: Risk transfers to the buyer as soon as goods are loaded aboard the vessel in Karachi, even though the seller pays freight to the destination port!
- **Best For:** Exporters seeking higher margin contracts by bundling competitive freight rates negotiated through their local forwarder.

### 4. DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) - Ultimate Door-to-Door Experience
- **The Setup:** The seller bears all risks and costs up to the buyer’s doorstep, including international transport, insurance, and foreign import duties/taxes (VAT/GST).

## 3. Practical Recommendations for Pakistani Traders

1. **Avoid EXW for First-Time Overseas Buyers:** You may lose control of export customs documentation, which can create difficulties in satisfying State Bank of Pakistan electronic Form E reconciliation requirements.
2. **Explicitly Specify the Port or Location:** Never write just "FOB". Always write: *"FOB Karachi Port Trust, Pakistan (Incoterms 2020)"* or *"CIF Port of Hamburg, Germany (Incoterms 2020)"*.
