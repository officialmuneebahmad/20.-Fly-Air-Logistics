Exporting goods from Pakistan to international markets requires a clear understanding of regulatory compliance, digital portal declarations, logistics consolidation, and carrier management. Whether you are shipping textiles from Faisalabad, surgical instruments from Sialkot, leather goods from Lahore, or agricultural produce from Multan, having a robust freight forwarding partner ensures seamless transit from factory floor to overseas destination.

## 1. Regulatory Prerequisites for Pakistani Exporters

Before booking any cargo with an international freight forwarder, every exporter registered in Pakistan must fulfill core institutional requirements:

- **National Tax Number (NTN) & Sales Tax Registration (STRN):** Active taxpayer status with the Federal Board of Revenue (FBR) is mandatory.
- **Pakistan Single Window (PSW) Subscription:** All foreign trade operations in Pakistan now interface through PSW, replacing individual manual processes with integrated digital customs processing.
- **WeBOC (Web Based One Customs) User ID:** Required for electronic filing of Goods Declarations (GD).
- **Chamber of Commerce Membership:** Active membership in your regional Chamber (e.g., Lahore Chamber LCCI, Karachi Chamber KCCI, Sialkot Chamber SCCI) to obtain certified Certificates of Origin.
- **Authorized Dealer (Bank) Registration:** Registering your export account with an authorized foreign exchange dealer bank for State Bank of Pakistan (SBP) foreign exchange remittance tracking.

## 2. Choosing the Right Exit Port and Transit Gateway

Pakistan features strategic sea and air cargo gateways:

### Sea Freight Terminals
- **Karachi Port Trust (KPT):** Historic port handling massive containerized (East and West Wharves) and breakbulk cargo.
- **Port Muhammad Bin Qasim (QICT / FOTCO):** Modern deep-water port hosting major liner services with dedicated terminal connectivity for mega container vessels.
- **Gwadar Port:** Pakistan's southwestern maritime gateway opening direct corridors to Central Asia and China under CPEC.

### Upcountry Dry Ports (Inland Container Depots)
For manufacturers based in Punjab and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, cargo does not need to travel via road unconsolidated. Inland Dry Ports offer customs clearance closer to the production base:
- **Lahore Dry Port (Mughalpura / Prem Nagar):** Primary hub for central Punjab industrial zones.
- **Sialkot Dry Port Trust (Sambrial):** Pioneer exporter-managed dry port specializing in air and sea consolidation for sports goods and surgical instruments.
- **Faisalabad Dry Port:** Dedicated to yarn, grey cloth, apparel, and home textiles.

### Air Cargo Terminals
- **Allama Iqbal International Airport (LHE), Lahore**
- **Jinnah International Airport (KHI), Karachi**
- **Islamabad International Airport (ISB)**
- **Sialkot International Airport (SKT)**

## 3. Mandatory Export Documentation Checklist

Accurate documentation prevents costly demurrage, carrier roll-overs, and customs holds:

1. **Commercial Invoice:** Specifying unit prices, total value, Incoterms (e.g., FOB, CIF, CFR), currency, and buyer details.
2. **Detailed Packing List:** Breaking down net weight, gross weight, carton dimensions, and CBM (Cubic Meters).
3. **Goods Declaration (GD):** Filed electronically via WeBOC / PSW under the Export regime.
4. **Electronic Form E / Financial Declaration:** Verified by the exporter's commercial bank under SBP regulations.
5. **Bill of Lading (B/L) or Air Waybill (AWB):** Issued by Fly Air Logistics upon receiving and loading the consignment.
6. **Certificate of Origin (COO):** Authenticated by the relevant Chamber of Commerce.
7. **Specialized Certificates:** Phytosanitary (for rice/fruits), Fumigation certificates (for wooden pallets conforming to ISPM-15), or CE marks.
